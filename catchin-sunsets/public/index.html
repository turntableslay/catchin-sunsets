The experience

Golden hour, turned up

Catchin' Sunsets takes over a rooftop for the best hours of the day. The DJ builds the energy, the drinks stay cold and everyone shows up dressed for the light. When the sun finally drops, the whole roof turns to watch it go. Then the party keeps going under the city lights.
